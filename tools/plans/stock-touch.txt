# Phase 1 for the Touch: what does the PC receive from a Touch on its STOCK profile?
# No flashing. input_capture.ps1 hooks the keyboard AND the mouse and logs every event
# (keys with the chord they form, cursor moves with deltas, wheel notches, button clicks)
# while you perform each gesture once, then says how many events of each kind arrived.
#
# Reference map: nayaos/docs/reference/module-profiles/naya-touch-windows.json (17 fields, the
# "Naya Touch Windows" stock profile). The expect= tokens below are that map: move, wheel,
# hwheel, click:left|right|middle, a chord such as Ctrl+Tab, or none; several separated by
# commas. If the map you pull off your module differs, edit the tokens to match.
#
# Before you start: park the mouse pointer over this console window or an empty patch of
# desktop, and keep your hands off the real mouse and keyboard while a capture is running.
# The hooks pass everything through, so taps are real clicks, 3-finger swipes switch windows
# and 4-finger swipes switch desktops; click back into this console whenever a gesture takes
# the focus away. The 4-finger tap opens Task View: press Esc to close it.
# Gestures marked expect=none (holds, pinch&spread) are recorded for 8 s from the moment you
# press Enter, since there is no first event to wait for; -NoneSeconds changes that.
#
# Redo just the two that were missed on 2026-09-09:
#       powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File tools\input_capture.ps1 -Only "pinch","HOLD still" -Out tools\plans\stock-touch.log -Append
#
# Run:  powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File tools\input_capture.ps1 -PlanFile tools\plans\stock-touch.txt -Seconds 4 -Out tools\plans\stock-touch.log
# Then: python tools\input_analyze.py tools\plans\stock-touch.log
# A section at a time: add -Only "3 fingers" (and -Append after the first run).
# Just watch the raw stream instead: powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File tools\input_capture.ps1 -Stream -Seconds 20

# --- taps: mouse buttons ---
tap (1 finger) expect=click:left
tap (2 fingers) expect=click:right
tap (3 fingers) expect=click:middle
tap (4 fingers) - opens Task View, press Esc afterwards expect=Win+Tab

# --- 1 finger: the cursor axes (vertical:1_finger, horizontal:1_finger) ---
1 finger: move up, about half the pad at a normal pace expect=move
1 finger: move down, about half the pad expect=move
1 finger: move left, about half the pad expect=move
1 finger: move right, about half the pad expect=move
1 finger: SLOW and LONG move up (the whole pad, about 1.5 s) expect=move
1 finger: FAST and SHORT flick up (about 1 cm) expect=move
1 finger: touch and HOLD still for 2 s without moving, then lift expect=none

# --- 2 fingers: the scroll axes (vertical:2_fingers, horizontal:2_fingers) and pinch ---
2 fingers: scroll up, about half the pad at a normal pace expect=wheel
2 fingers: scroll down, about half the pad expect=wheel
2 fingers: scroll left, about half the pad expect=hwheel
2 fingers: scroll right, about half the pad expect=hwheel
2 fingers: SLOW and LONG scroll up (the whole pad, about 1.5 s) expect=wheel
2 fingers: FAST and SHORT flick up (about 1 cm) expect=wheel
2 fingers: scroll up, then HOLD both fingers still for 2 s before lifting expect=wheel
2 fingers: pinch, then spread back out (one pinch&spread axis; stock binds nothing to it) expect=none

# --- 3 fingers: window-switching chords (focus moves; click back into this console after each) ---
3 fingers: swipe up expect=Shift+Alt+Esc
3 fingers: swipe down expect=Alt+Esc
3 fingers: swipe left expect=Ctrl+Shift+Tab
3 fingers: swipe right expect=Ctrl+Tab
3 fingers: SLOW and LONG swipe right (the whole pad, about 1.5 s - does the chord repeat?) expect=Ctrl+Tab
3 fingers: FAST and SHORT flick right (about 1 cm) expect=Ctrl+Tab

# --- 4 fingers: desktop chords (Ctrl+Win+Left/Right only switch if you have more than one virtual desktop) ---
4 fingers: swipe up expect=Alt+PgUp
4 fingers: swipe down expect=Alt+PgDn
4 fingers: swipe left expect=Ctrl+Win+Left
4 fingers: swipe right expect=Ctrl+Win+Right
4 fingers: SLOW and LONG swipe up (does the chord repeat?) expect=Alt+PgUp

# --- not in the stock map: does the firmware send anything for these at all? ---
double tap (1 finger) - stock has no double-tap field, so expect two plain clicks expect=click:left
1 finger: a quick flick up then lift - is there a swipe event separate from the cursor moves? expect=move
2 fingers: tap then immediately swipe up (a tap-and-drag) expect=click:right,wheel
